# Full ontology representation

fwoosh does not model the data as a full ontology. That was the original
ambition, but it is larger than the time available; fwoosh deliberately stops
at the Ossie semantic-model level - datasets, fields, metrics, relationships -
and leaves richer ontological modeling out of scope.
